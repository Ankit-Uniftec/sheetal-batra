# DIVERGENCES

Every cluster from the `.duplication/*.md` sweeps that was tagged **NUMBER-AFFECTING**
or **DIVERGENCE**. These are **not** extraction/refactor tasks — each is a latent bug or
an unresolved product/tax/access decision where two or more screens compute or gate on the
*same* concept differently, so the *same order* can show different numbers, labels, columns,
or access depending on which screen you look at.

**No fixes are proposed here.** The point is to catalogue the disagreements and route each to
whoever owns the decision. Once a decision lands, the mechanical collapse lives back in the
`.duplication/*.md` files (the TIER1/2/3/4 notes) — not here.

Owner legend: **me** = engineering/UX call (which vocabulary/scope/label wins);
**accountant** = money & tax correctness (GST rate/base/rounding, payment buckets);
**Jahnavi** = product rules (measurement field sets, urgency signals, discount stacking, period semantics).
